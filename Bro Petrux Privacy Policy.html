Privacy Policy for Bro Petrux

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to Bro Petrux ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data when you play our mobile game.

By using our game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect Automatically (Third-Party SDKs)

Our game does not collect any personal data directly through our own servers, and we do not require you to create an internal game account. However, we integrate trusted third-party services from Google to provide essential game features, processing, and advertising. These services automatically collect specific data types:

  • User IDs: We use Google Play Services for sign-in, achievements, and leaderboard features. This service accesses your Google Play Games User ID to save and sync your game progress securely.
  • Financial Information (Purchase History): We use Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. We access your purchase history to process transactions, unlock items, and restore purchases. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details.
  • App Activity & Interactions: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob collects data regarding your interactions with the game and ads (such as ad clicks, views, and taps) to optimize ad performance.
  • App Info, Performance, and Diagnostics: We collect Crash logs and Diagnostics data through Google services to monitor app performance, fix bugs, and ensure technical stability.
  • Device or Other IDs: Google AdMob utilizes your Android Advertising ID to deliver relevant, personalized advertisements and for fraud prevention.

2. How We Use the Data

The data collected by third-party SDKs is used solely for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To process inside-game purchases and maintain core game features.
  • Account Management: To track your game progression, achievements, and ranking via Google Play Games.
  • Advertising & Analytics: To display advertisements, measure ad campaigns, and analyze game stability.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To protect against fraudulent transactions and technical abuse.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties. Data sharing occurs only with Google’s official services (Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob) to manage advertising and core game features as described above.

You can learn more about how Google handles your data by visiting the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

4. Data Encryption and Security

We value your trust. All data transmitted between the game and Google's servers is fully encrypted in transit using secure protocols such as HTTPS/TLS, ensuring that your data cannot be intercepted by unauthorized parties.

5. User Controls and Data Deletion

  • Account Deletion: Because our game uses existing external Google accounts and does not allow users to create an independent account within the app, we do not store your credentials on any external private server.
  • Opting Out: You can reset or turn off personalized ads at any time by navigating to your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads), and choosing to reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of ad personalization.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us.
