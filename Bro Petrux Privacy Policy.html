Privacy Policy for Bro Petrux

Last updated: September 2026

RgamesID Studios operates the game "Bro Petrux". This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you play our game via Steam and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information or personal data from our players directly. The game runs locally on your PC device and does not require a custom user account registration with us.

2. Steam Platform Data

Our game is distributed through Valve's Steam platform. Steam may collect gameplay statistics, achievements, and technical crash logs to process your experience. For more information regarding how Steam handles your data, please review the Steam Privacy Agreement.

3. Log Data and Local Saves

The game may create temporary log data or local save files directly on your computer's storage to preserve game progress, settings, and graphics configurations. This data stays entirely on your local device and is never uploaded to our external servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you have any concerns regarding children's privacy, please feel free to reach out to us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at our official developer platform.
